# Theory index

The historical [theoretical framework](BTP_Theoretical_Framework.tex) and [PDF](BTP_Theoretical_Framework.pdf) remain unchanged. The report's later theoretical development is collected in the post-experiment supplement dated 2026-10-31: [readable proofs](PROOFS.md), [LaTeX source](proofs.tex), and [PDF](proofs.pdf). The supplement gives 27 numbered statements, using their report labels as stable IDs. Its results are conditional, and do not certify experimental outcomes or claim originality for standard arguments. The fallback is theoretical and was not the frozen selection method.

Use the historical [T-label statement register](statements.md), [assumptions](assumptions.md), [notation](notation.md), and [proof-to-experiment map](proof_to_experiment_map.csv) to connect the original themes to later results. These T labels do not renumber or replace the 27 supplement IDs. The [proof review](PROOF_REVIEW.md) records clarifications and limitations. The historical backlog records targets at its own time; a later proof of a conditional special case does not establish its assumptions in the experiments.

The model-expectation objective differs from the measured loss of a finite generated table. Finite-query bounds do not automatically give population total variation guarantees, and numerical checks are not proofs. See [chronology](post_experiment_additions.md).

Exact-model size optimality concerns the declared population mixture and its population KL (equivalently expected log likelihood), under exact component models. A multinomial MLE for observed client labels is a separate result. Neither establishes that size weights maximize the observed-data mixture likelihood for every finite sample; the supplement gives a counterexample.
