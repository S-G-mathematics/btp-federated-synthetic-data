# Assumptions and boundaries

The original framework and the [supplement](PROOFS.md) distinguish a model-answer objective from finite-table loss. Client generators are fixed when taking their expectations; the reference is a declared empirical table, and the queries are fixed, measurable and bounded in [0,1]. The feasible weights lie in the simplex. A finite pilot controls the objective only on a valid simultaneous error event for all client/query pairs and the reference summary; an unknown radius is not a certificate. Independent fresh generated rows and fixed or conditionally fixed queries are required for the stated sampling bounds. Adaptive query discovery or repeated unaccounted selection requires further control.

Conditional transfer requires positive mass and controls for the condition indicator and the appropriate joint queries. Exact projection masses require integrated or summed preimages, not merely an evaluation at a projected row. Prediction risk transfer requires control of its loss class, which the finite synthesis workload does not automatically provide. Finite-support log-score bounds require positive comparable scores; the transport result uses its stated KL-to-product regularization and explicit support, cost, and optimization gaps.

Exact size optimality assumes correct client models and target masses. Approximate size bounds assume local query-error bounds, and localization also requires query distinguishability; the sufficient dual certificate requires its displayed active-query weights and has not been verified for the campaign's exact H. The fallback additionally needs a valid uncertainty event and fresh final tables; it is a post-experiment theoretical proposal, not the frozen selection algorithm.

A privacy composition conclusion applies only when every component mechanism satisfies its stated neighboring relation and privacy conditions. The four FLAIM fits have a conditional mechanism-specific approximate-DP interpretation; the main pipeline is nonprivate. See [proof review](PROOF_REVIEW.md), [theory clarifications](../docs/THEORY_CLARIFICATIONS.txt), and [release ledger](../docs/FINAL_RELEASE_AND_PRIVACY_LEDGER.md). No population-TV error is measured by the finite-query results.
