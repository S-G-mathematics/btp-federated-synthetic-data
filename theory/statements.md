# Formal statement and interpretation register

The [29-section original framework](BTP_Theoretical_Framework.tex) is a dated research framework. A displayed identity or programme there is not automatically a proved new theorem. `D_R` and `D_S` are observed datasets; an unknown population law in the framework is conceptual and was not measured. The finite-query statement in [`docs/FINITE_QUERY_TRANSFER_PROPOSITION_20261025.md`](../docs/FINITE_QUERY_TRANSFER_PROPOSITION_20261025.md) is explicitly dated after development. Chronology of any other later addition that cannot be proven from source timestamps is labelled **later/uncertain**, never retrospectively prospective. The [theory-only backlog](../AUTHOR_READY/THEORY_ONLY_BACKLOG.md) records historical targets, not the current status of every target. The post-experiment [27-statement supplement](PROOFS.md) supplies conditional proofs and counterexamples; its label IDs are separate from the historical T labels below. See the [review](PROOF_REVIEW.md) for assumptions and limits.
