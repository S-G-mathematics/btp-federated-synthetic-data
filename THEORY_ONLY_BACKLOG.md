# Theory-only backlog after bounded experimental closure

These are mathematical tasks, not hidden requests to rerun or select experiments. They are not needed to state the measured Adult/Bank comparative findings.

1. **Finite-sample fallback guarantee.** Let `alpha` be fixed, `W_{delta,c}` the preregistered representation-feasible simplex, `H` a synthetic pilot estimate and `b` an allowed real-summary estimate. Define a rule that selects calibrated weights only when a simultaneous, selection-aware upper confidence bound on the *fresh-sample* empirical query-max improvement over `alpha` is below a practical negative margin; otherwise return `alpha`. Prove a correct bound under explicit independent pilot/reference sampling and (if used) private-noise assumptions, including adaptive search across candidate methods and finite synthesis rows. A generic two-times-estimation-error inequality alone is not a novelty result.
2. **Target-representation impossibility and sharpness.** Characterize when `||H(w-alpha)||_infinity <= ||w-alpha||_1/2` is tight and construct counterexamples in which a query gain under `w` is solely a change in client mass while a different unmeasured query worsens. This elementary inequality is already established; any publishable theorem needs substantive new assumptions or conclusions.
3. **Projected score exactness.** For a discrete raw joint `Q` and deterministic projection `T`, formally characterize when evaluating `Q` at `T(z)` equals the pushforward `Q_T(z)=sum_{u:T(u)=z}Q(u)`. Extend the small education-number enumeration proof to a general finite-support class, and characterize which approximate copula/GMM scores can support calibrated conditional probabilities after rounding/clipping. Do not call an approximate density route exact without a common base measure and preimage accounting.
4. **Conditional transfer under limited queries.** Tighten the finite-query ratio bound for registered conditions when real and synthetic denominators are estimated with finite samples, and include teacher-to-student approximation and support-gate error. State low-support/vacuous regimes and a counterexample showing that matching an incomplete workload does not control every conditional query or predictive loss.
5. **Operator approximation.** Bound finite support/smoothing/optimization error for geometric pooling and entropic transport barycenters under their actual discrete candidates. Separate support ceiling from transport regularization and projection error. No exact continuous barycenter theorem follows from the current finite solver.
6. **Privacy composition, if a future private method is proposed.** Formalize adjacency, client-membership mechanism, data-dependent partitioning, count releases, model training, summary selection and repeated overlapping seeds. The isolated public-bin histogram result and the adapted FLAIM accountant do not compose into a private BTP pipeline.

The proofs should cite the saved counterexamples and empirical objects, but experimental observations are not mathematical proof. Human academic review of novelty and publication suitability remains separate.
