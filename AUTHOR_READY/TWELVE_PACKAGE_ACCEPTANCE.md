## Exact evidence and scope

### Experiment 1: Does weighting improve fixed-target empirical fidelity?

- **Result:** 0/18 Adult development and 0/6 confirmation partition/K slices reached -0.005; overall confirmation delta -0.000215
- **Completed scope:** 54 corrected Adult development cells; 12 frozen confirmation cells; same locally tuned models and five 10k draws
- **Excluded:** Other populations, workloads and privacy-matched aggregation; forbidden: A general weighting-improvement or equivalence claim
- **Status:** `COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING`
- **Exact evidence:**
  - `docs/ADULT_WEIGHTING_DEVELOPMENT_CLOSURE.md`
  - `docs/FROZEN_CONFIRMATION_FINDINGS.md`
  - `reports/final_build/confirmation_slices.csv`

### Experiment 2: How close are candidates to a finite-pilot oracle?

- **Result:** Finite matrix optimum certified; larger pilots reduced numerical excess but did not establish practical fresh-sample gain
- **Completed scope:** 57 certified fixed-pilot LPs; six-cell four-size/five-seed pilot sensitivity
- **Excluded:** Population optimum or globally optimal nonlinear operators; forbidden: Population or continuous-pooling optimality
- **Status:** `COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING`
- **Exact evidence:**
  - `docs/PROJECTED_PILOT_SENSITIVITY_FINDINGS_20261024.md`
  - `reports/projected_pilot_sensitivity_v2_20261024/`

### Experiment 3: Do overlapping client subsets add mixture expressivity?

- **Result:** Mixtures of subset mixtures collapse to one global mixture
- **Completed scope:** Cyclic subset effective-weight identity and saved numerical check
- **Excluded:** New within-subset estimators; forbidden: Novel subset generator from regrouping
- **Status:** `CLOSED_NEGATIVE_OR_INAPPLICABLE`
- **Exact evidence:**
  - `docs/EXPERIMENTS_12.md`
  - `docs/WEIGHT_METHODS.md`
  - `results/ec2_fullstage_dev_20261024/copula_age_k5_s101_24ff1a5a4b33acfa/exp03_subsets.json`

### Experiment 4: What changes with synthetic sample size?

- **Result:** Draw variation shrank; model error persisted; no practical constrained gain
- **Completed scope:** Six checkpoints, m=2500/10000/40000, five draws each
- **Excluded:** Other families or very large m; forbidden: Universal population error floor
- **Status:** `COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING`
- **Exact evidence:**
  - `docs/PROJECTED_SAMPLE_SIZE_FINDINGS_20261024.md`
  - `reports/projected_sample_size_20261024/`

### Experiment 5: When does conditional synthesis succeed or abstain?

- **Result:** Direct teacher default; older-doctorate request abstained 12/12. Female-condition relationship=Husband was the worst query in all 12 confirmation cells: zero alpha-standardized real frequency, generated 0.340?0.399, mean absolute error 0.375 despite zero condition violations.
- **Completed scope:** Six development student/direct-teacher cells; frozen 12-cell four-condition confirmation
- **Excluded:** Arbitrary-condition fidelity certification or learned cross-condition generalization; forbidden: Universal conditional accuracy
- **Status:** `COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING`
- **Exact evidence:**
  - `docs/PROJECTED_CONDITIONAL_FINDINGS_20261024.md`
  - `docs/FROZEN_CONFIRMATION_FINDINGS.md`
  - `reports/final_build/confirmation_conditional.csv`

### Experiment 6: Does synthetic training preserve income probability utility?

- **Result:** At registered unequal budgets, size logistic Brier 0.129818 versus privileged TRTR 0.101987 and AUROC 0.856167 versus 0.904646. TRTR used 21,148/4,880/3,254 real train/tune/calibration rows versus 10,000/2,000/2,000 generated rows; no matched-size effect inferred.
- **Completed scope:** Paired logistic/extra-trees, synthetic TSTR and privileged real TRTR, six development and 12 confirmation cells
- **Excluded:** Independent cohort utility and small unsupported subgroups; forbidden: Synthetic-over-real superiority
- **Status:** `COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING`
- **Exact evidence:**
  - `docs/FROZEN_CONFIRMATION_FINDINGS.md`
  - `reports/final_build/confirmation_utility.csv`

### Experiment 7: Can a joint model imply an income predictor?

- **Result:** Observed-row coverage 100%; exact-score preimages audited; approximate routes labelled
- **Completed scope:** Six same-person projected development comparisons; exact finite Chow-Liu route and approximate GMM route
- **Excluded:** Universal off-support coverage and exact copula/GMM probabilities; forbidden: Exact rounded/clipped continuous score claim
- **Status:** `COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING`
