## 4. Implemented generators and aggregation comparisons

### Local client generators

| Generator | Statistical form | Score/release qualification |
|---|---|---|
| independent | Smoothed mixed categorical and numeric-bin marginals | Exact declared discretized integer mass, including within-bin integer probability |
| chow_liu | Dependency tree over categorical values and numeric bins | Exact declared discretized integer mass |
| copula | Gaussian copula with empirical marginals | Approximate mixed-data score; nominal category ordering matters |
| mixed_gmm | Train-standardized Gaussian mixture with component categorical probabilities, optional log1p heavy-tail transforms and covariance regularization | Approximate score after integer rounding/clipping |
| prototype_kde | Compressed center/kernel mixture with component categorical probabilities | Approximate score; released centers can still disclose information; weak first pilot |
| latent_class | EM mixture of product distributions over declared bins/vocabularies | Exact declared discretized integer mass |

The two extra families, prototype_kde and latent_class, were implemented in btp/models_extra.py and connected through btp/models.py. Each family has a six-setting pilot/full local tuning grid. Local tune data choose settings; per-client tuning tables, checkpoints, counts, convergence/finite-score checks and timings are saved. The GMM grid was expanded after local-tune sensitivity: 12/16 components improved local tune in all five clients of the inspected age/K=5 case. That justified a development rerun, not a claim that capacity is optimal. The expanded GMM rerun reduced assessment query max from roughly 0.099 to 0.056 in one age/K=5 split. Exact and approximate score kinds remain separate.

### Eight candidate statistical families

F1 predictive-score weighting; F2 conditional predictive-evidence weighting over frozen candidates; F3 log-score stacking; F4 moment/entropy/query/GLS calibration variants; F5 generator-level random-feature kernel mean matching; F6 uncertainty-adjusted robust query calibration; F7 finite synthetic-support logarithmic pooling; and F8 finite entropic transport pooling were run with uniform and sample-size mixture baselines. These are comparison families, **not eight invented algorithms**. F1/F2 can coincide under a uniform prior and unit temperature. F6 is not predictive Agnostic Federated Learning. F7/F8 are finite-support approximations with explicit support, floor/regularization and search limitations. The empirical Exp 2 oracle is an LP optimum only for its fixed synthetic pilot matrix and real validation query vector, not a population optimum.

F1/F2/F3 currently consume real per-record calibration log-score evaluations in the privileged evaluator. They are marked privileged and must not be compared as equal-access deployable model-only protocols. Other server fits receive released local models, synthetic pilots, declared metadata and permitted aggregate calibration query/kernel summaries. Those unnoised summaries and the released client models are nonprivate. The release boundary is a single-process research simulation, not a secure network deployment. Evidence: docs/WEIGHT_METHODS.md, docs/RELEASE_CONTRACT.md and each cell's releases/release_contract.json.

## 23. Finite-query conditional transfer and student accounting (25 October 2026 local time)

I fixed a six-cell Experiment 11 protocol and wrote an elementary proposition with proof and a binary counterexample in docs/FINITE_QUERY_TRANSFER_PROPOSITION_20261025.md. For empirical alpha-standardized real R, synthetic teacher S, condition A of real mass r>0 and synthetic mass s>0, the declared bounded-query conditional error is at most the joint-intersection query error plus condition-mass error, divided by r. This is deterministic finite-query algebra, not a new population theorem. If s=0 the synthetic conditional is undefined; if r is tiny the bound can be nearly one. Matching only one-way marginals can coexist with a complete reversal of an unmeasured conditional.

The EC2 audit regenerated 30,000-row teachers from the saved local models for size and constrained weights on Chow-Liu age/K5 and mixed-GMM label-skew/K10 at seeds101/202/303.
