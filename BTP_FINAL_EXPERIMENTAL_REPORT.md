## Abstract

We tested whether permitted statistical calibration of non-neural client generators improves empirical synthetic-data fidelity over size weighting while preserving a fixed client-population target. Official UCI Adult supplied 32,561 training and 16,281 official-test rows. A corrected projected development matrix comprised 54 matched copula, Chow–Liu and mixed Gaussian-mixture cells, three independent controls, 741 method rows, and five synthetic draws per method/cell. Under `||w−alpha||_1≤0.25`, `w_i≥0.5 alpha_i` and a registered 0.005 practical query-max margin, zero of 18 development slices improved practically; mean constrained-minus-size error was +0.002428. Validation selected one projected copula size-weighted procedure, which was frozen before official test. Its 12-cell official confirmation gave overall size query-max 0.022216 and constrained-minus-size −0.000215; zero of six partition/K slices reached the margin. A 12-cell UCI Bank Marketing replication likewise had zero of four practical-gain slices. Secondary utility and conditional findings exposed limits beyond marginal query fidelity. The main model release is nonprivate and, in three measured cells, larger than a comparably serialized raw table. The defensible conclusion is a bounded negative comparison with a specified size-weight fallback, not equivalence, impossibility, end-to-end privacy, or broad population generalization.

## 1. Estimand and design

Every client holds the same predictor/label schema. Client `i` fits a non-neural joint generator `Q_i` from its own training rows and releases that model plus specifically permitted metadata. The target mass is fixed at `alpha_i = n_i / sum_j n_j` from declared client training counts. A method's synthesis weight `w_i` changes its generator contribution but never redefines `alpha`. For a registered query `q`, the primary real answer is the **alpha-standardized empirical** client reference `sum_i alpha_i mean_{D_i^reference} q`; an ordinary pooled held-out answer is saved separately. The synthetic answer is the mean over exactly `m=10,000` generated rows. The primary loss is `max_q |b_S(q)-b_R(q)|` over `Q_primary_v2`. Differences are candidate minus tuned projected size, so negative favours the candidate. Adult `fnlwgt` remains an ordinary predictor, not a survey expansion weight. This is measured dataset-to-dataset discrepancy, not unknown-population TV.

Adult official training duplicates of official test were quarantined before development splitting (25 rows), with remaining full-record duplicate groups kept together. Train/local tune/calibration/validation/development assessment were separately assigned; assignment rules and preprocessing were fit only from permitted early roles, then fixed for held-out roles. No official test row or confirmation seed entered method selection. Public bounds/vocab and structural projection are documented in the release contract. The privileged evaluator could access real held-out rows and candidate per-record scores; deployment-style summary methods received only allowed models, pilots and aggregate releases. F1–F3 predictive scoring used privileged common-real calibration access, which is not equated to model-only access. The main pipeline is nonprivate.

Local model families were independent marginals, integer-bin Chow–Liu, empirical-marginal Gaussian copula and mixed GMM; latent class and KDE explorations are separately versioned. Local six-setting grids and tune criterion are in the immutable freeze and model cards. Exact integer-bin likelihood includes within-bin mass. Chow–Liu's projected joint probability was checked by summing deterministic-projection preimages; copula and rounded/clipped GMM scores remain approximate and are not compared across different base measures. Projection repairs education/education-number on generated Adult rows; structural-zero diagnosis includes capital columns. These corrections were assessment informed and are transparently labelled exploratory development.

The corrected Adult development core covered three generators, age/age+education/label skews, K5/K10 and seeds101/202/303, with five synthesis repeats. The final generator selector used **only validation** primary loss for projected size weighting, equal mean across 18 cells per family and average of five draws within each. Copula scored 0.029975 versus Chow–Liu 0.031455 and mixed GMM 0.051446. The selection rule's 0.001 tie window did not apply. `w=alpha`, deterministic largest-remainder allocation, tuning grid, workload, conditional support, predictive calibration and confirmation commands were then frozen globally. The freeze SHA-256 is `2420f737b6af732925f9cce3fbb82c8a9a7a30e1988dde5e68900d1404bb3a23`.

## 3. Mechanisms, conditional synthesis and prediction

Repeated pilot experiments at four sizes/five seeds and synthetic-size curves at 2,500/10,000/40,000 rows separated pilot/sampling variation from persistent model error. Six-cell paired downstream studies, 12 same-row projection checkpoint contrasts, six release-summary ablations and six fixed-quantity heterogeneity cells examined why error moved. The controlled heterogeneity experiment fixed K5 client training/tune quantity at 1,000/200, compared severity 0.25/0.85 and used a separate equal-client target; it does not isolate every causal feature of assignment. Pure mixtures of overlapping subset mixtures reduce to an effective global mixture; no extra within-subset expressive model is claimed.

Official-test paired probability predictions for logistic and extra-trees were trained and calibrated using separate source-specific roles. In every confirmation cell, privileged real TRTR used **21,148 train / 4,880 tune / 3,254 calibration** rows; each synthetic TSTR procedure used **10,000 train / 2,000 tune / 2,000 calibration** generated rows. Size synthetic logistic averaged **Brier 0.129818 / AUROC 0.856167**, versus privileged real TRTR **0.101987 / 0.904646**; extra-trees similarly favoured TRTR (Brier 0.133218 versus 0.105741). These are registered but unequal training budgets, so the finding does not establish an intrinsic matched-size disadvantage of synthetic training. Constrained synthetic logistic Brier was 0.129254, a descriptive small change relative to size. The same held-out people were scored within each cell. Unsupported small-group AUROC is flagged rather than fabricated. Exact projected Chow–Liu joint-implied probabilities, approximate GMM/copula routes and a separately trained synthetic predictor were compared on matched development people; no exact mixed continuous posterior claim survives.

The deployable conditional default uses the selected teacher with structured public-schema Boolean parsing, a separate 30,000-row support pilot, minimum 300 matches and 1% empirical pilot mass, direct rejection to 1,000 accepted features and a 200,000-proposal cap. It abstains if support or proposal limits fail. In frozen Adult confirmation, three common registered conditions succeeded in all 12 cells with zero accepted-condition violations. Mean within-condition feature-query max was **0.08379** for age25–45, **0.37500** for female and **0.14701** for bachelors+age30–60. For the female condition, `relationship=Husband` was the worst saved feature query in every cell: the alpha-standardized real conditional frequency was zero in these reference records and generated frequency ranged **0.340–0.399**. This is an observed sex/relationship dependency failure in the frozen model, not a universal demographic rule. Zero condition violations show only that the requested filter held; they do not certify other features. The older-doctorate request abstained 12/12. Previously trained per-condition Chow–Liu students remain bounded alternatives; their raw condition violation and within-condition errors were retained. Support is a service heuristic, not a certified probability or fidelity lower bound. Elementary finite-query ratio and student/direct triangle checks are documented in Experiment 11; rare denominators can make bounds vacuous.

## 5. Evidence, uncertainty and limitations

The report builder `scripts/build_final_reports.py` consumes saved artifacts and emits `reports/final_build/summary.json`, per-draw/cell/slice/query/utility/conditional CSVs and static scientific figures. `ALL_RUNS_INDEX.csv/json` separates historical, corrected current, external, reference and confirmation evidence; failed attempts remain in `ALL_FAILURES_AND_REPAIRS.md`. The clean-room check regenerated a confirmation size draw byte-identically (SHA-256 `973e04cbd4e9845031ffc2d30532e6102a0beaa5ab4bfb75e0e0bd9ff498c30e`) and reconstructed its maximum named query. The pre-freeze suite had **91 passing tests**. Tests and reproducibility establish software/artifact consistency, not population validity.

Adult test people recur across partition/seed configurations, Bank contacts may recur, and five draws estimate synthetic sampling conditional on fixed models. No p-value treating those draws as independent populations is reported. The main maximum is nonsmooth and limited to 152 registered primary queries; audit queries were viewed during development. Real sampling, assignment, model fit, pilot, draw, optimization and DP-noise uncertainty are distinct. Alpha mass constraints do not by themselves guarantee observable subgroup fidelity. Copula's nominal ordering and approximate scores, residual model misspecification, supported-condition errors and payload inflation limit deployment claims. The empirical thesis is a rigorous bounded negative comparison and size-weight fallback. General method superiority, end-to-end DP, arbitrary-query/condition transfer and publication novelty are not established.
