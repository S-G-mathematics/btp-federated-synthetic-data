# Thesis draft: empirical methods, results and limitations

## Methods

The scientific estimand is a fixed client-mixture of empirical held-out data, standardized to training client masses `alpha`. Each client fits a non-neural joint distribution locally. A synthesis rule receives released fitted models and specific aggregate calibration statistics, selects weights `w` and allocates exactly 10,000 rows by largest remainder. Size weighting fixes `w=alpha`; the registered constrained comparator limits L1 mass change to 0.25 and requires every client to receive at least half its target mass. The primary score is the maximum absolute synthetic-versus-alpha-standardized real answer over a registered 152-query Adult workload. It is an empirical finite-query loss, not a population-divergence estimator. The practical improvement margin was 0.005.

Official UCI Adult was separated into development roles from official training and a locked 16,281-row official test. Twenty-five official-training exact duplicates of test records were quarantined; remaining duplicate groups were assigned intact. All partition rules, local tuning and preprocessing were fit using permitted training/local-tune roles and applied unchanged later. Corrected development compared three locally tuned family candidates across three partition skews, K5/K10 and three seeds, five synthetic draws per cell. Validation chose a single projected copula family using size weighting. The complete selection, conditional service and predictive policies were written in `SELECTION_FROZEN.json` before the first official-test **confirmation-scoring** access; raw official-test records were read earlier for source validation and duplicate quarantine without scoring. A separate official UCI Bank additional-full dataset replication used a prospectively frozen 12-cell protocol, but repeated contacts can involve the same person. Unnoised aggregate summaries make the main pipeline nonprivate. PGM and FLAIM reference trust/access differences were kept explicit.

## Results

In corrected Adult development, constrained calibration failed the 0.005 threshold in all 18 generator/partition/K slices; mean constrained-minus-size query-max was +0.002428. In frozen Adult confirmation, size achieved 0.022216 overall and constrained calibration changed it by −0.000215; no six-slice practical improvement occurred. The external Bank mean change was −0.001332 with no four-slice practical improvement. A finite-pilot LP was certified for each corrected Adult core/control cell, but certifies only its finite matrix. Same-row projection, summary-access and fixed-quantity skew contrasts supplied bounded error-mechanism evidence. Increasing generated sample count reduced Monte Carlo variability without repairing all fitted-model bias.

At registered unequal budgets, official-test real-trained logistic probabilities achieved Brier 0.101987 and AUROC 0.904646; synthetic-size logistic achieved 0.129818 and 0.856167. Real TRTR used 21,148/4,880/3,254 train/tune/calibration rows versus 10,000/2,000/2,000 generated TSTR rows; no matched-size effect is inferred. Direct teacher conditional rejection satisfied accepted conditions, but its female-condition `relationship=Husband` frequency was 0.340–0.399 against zero in the alpha-standardized real female reference, giving mean feature-query maximum 0.375; rare older-doctorate requests abstained 12/12. This is a measured dependency failure of the frozen model, not a universal demographic rule. Four same-summary PGM development references and four adapted FLAIM private references were mixed. The four FLAIM releases compose to `(epsilon, delta)=(16, 4e-6)` under the stated per-fit and fixed-membership assumptions only; the separate epsilon1/4/8 Laplace histogram releases compose to pure epsilon13 under their own conditional assumptions. Three measured copula model-plus-metadata releases exceeded comparably encoded raw-table bytes. The main BTP pipeline remains nonprivate.

## Limitations and interpretation

The Adult official people recur across seeds and partitions; five draws measure synthesis variability conditional on checkpoints rather than independent study cohorts. Development protocol choices were informed by earlier assessment, although final generator selection used validation only. Bank Marketing rows may represent repeated contacts. The primary 152 queries can miss unmeasured dependencies. Copula nominal coding and approximate mixed scores limit exact posterior claims. Zero client mass is not identical to zero observable subgroup coverage, while small L1 mass shift is not a fairness proof. FLAIM's interactive private raw-client contract differs from BTP's unnoised model release, and HeteroFedSyn was not numerically matched. Conditional support does not imply conditional fidelity. The empirical contribution is an audited bounded negative comparison and a specified size fallback under a declared target and access budget. Formal fallback guarantees, full privacy and broad transfer are separate work.
