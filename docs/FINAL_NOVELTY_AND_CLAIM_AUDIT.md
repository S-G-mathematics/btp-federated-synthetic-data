# Final novelty and claim audit — 25 October 2026

The surviving BTP contribution is a **bounded empirical comparison**, not an invented eighth weighting family or a private federated generator. Its central question is when released statistical information can improve empirical synthetic-data query fidelity over a properly tuned, schema-corrected `w=alpha` mixture **without moving the fixed client-population target**. The registered Adult development search found no practical constrained-weighting gain; the Bank external replication likewise found no three-seed slice reaching the transferred 0.005 margin. Frozen official Adult confirmation completed after the written gate: overall constrained-minus-size primary difference was −0.000215 and no partition/K slice reached the practical −0.005 margin. It supports the bounded size fallback, without proving equivalence or impossibility.

The current empirical distinction is precise: fixed alpha, explicit access tracks, strong local non-neural tuning, public structural projection, representation-constrained versus unconstrained weights, independent generated-pilot and draw variation, support-limited conditional service, and a registered size fallback. It is a comparative design that may be useful even with null/adverse results. The simple bound `||H(w-alpha)||_infinity <= ||w-alpha||_1/2` for `[0,1]` query columns and the finite-query conditional ratio bound are elementary explanations, not original theorems. No predictive AFL, population TV, universal conditional generalization, compressed payload or end-to-end DP claim is retained.

The same-access maintained PGM reference is mixed in four Adult development cells (one small improvement, three worse than size). The adapted FLAIM utility reference is also mixed, with one better and three worse than the **nonprivate** size reference, while receiving a different raw-client/privacy contract. Adult and Bank development results support an honest negative comparative thesis, not a general impossibility result. A broad transfer or publication-novelty assertion would require independent datasets, a matched trust/privacy baseline and/or a genuinely distinct estimator with a nontrivial proved guarantee.
