## 9A: same raw draws before and after public projection

Each method's raw model samples were generated once and the education-number mapping applied to those **same rows**. The primary query-max fell from about **0.268–0.328 to 0.028–0.033** for Chow-Liu, and from about **0.693–0.708 to 0.049–0.055** for mixed GMM across the registered age/K5 and label-skew/K10 slices. The raw mapped education-number violation rate was about **0.267–0.327** for Chow-Liu and **0.693–0.708** for GMM; it became zero after projection. Detailed paired draw means for size and constrained weights are in `reports/mechanisms/projection_summary.csv`, with per-draw losses and relation rates in `projection_paired_draws.csv` and `projection_relation.csv`.

This is an isolated sampling transformation effect for fixed local models, rows and weights. It explains a dominant historical structural defect and why raw/unprojected campaigns cannot be pooled with the corrected projected core. It does **not** prove that projection improves every unconstrained data statistic: the primary maximum includes the public relation query, and the transformation can move other answers. Nor does it show a novel weighting advantage. The exact projected Chow-Liu probability claim remains supported by the separate pushforward log-sum-exp audit; approximate GMM score claims remain approximate.

## 9B: released query subset access

For each of six saved copula cells (age+education/K5 and label-skew/K10, seeds101/202/303), the same fixed pilot and calibration summary were restricted to 143 one-way queries, 151 one/two-way queries or the full 152-query primary workload. The full extra query is the education-number relation; projected pilots and real targets already satisfy it at zero, so its LP solution and observed losses exactly coincide with the 151-query variant in all six cells. That equality is a substantive null ablation, not evidence that arbitrary higher-order information never helps.

Mean paired primary query-max differences versus size across the six cells were **+0.000208** (one-way) and **+0.001502** (one/two-way and full). Individual cells include both favourable and adverse differences; for example, one age+education seed202 two-way variant was +0.009655 while another age+education seed303 was -0.002051. The representation constraint, optimization solver and five draw seeds were fixed. The access variants differ in unnoised summary query count/payload bytes (`reports/mechanisms/summary_release_bytes.csv`); they are not equal-information competitors. No GLS covariance or regularization component is claimed for this retained constrained minimax rule.

**Disposition:** COMPLETE_BOUNDED_FINDING. Structural projection removes a large measured support/relationship defect; adding the registered two-way summary set did not establish a reproducible constrained-weighting gain here. Broader interaction-set design, score-access variants, and joint-to-student effects are outside this isolated Exp9 closure and are not used as retained positive claims.
