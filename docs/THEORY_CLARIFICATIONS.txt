3. The exact discrete generators in code assign probability to every sampled integer, not just a numeric bin: bin mass is divided by the count of integers in that bin. 4. Eight candidate families from the weighting discussion are not eight novel algorithms. Moment calibration and kernel mean matching use distinct features but can share a constrained least-squares solver. Conditional fixed-model predictive-evidence BMA and pseudo-BMA can coincide for uniform model priors and temperature 1. Classical Bayesian integration over learned parameter posteriors is not implemented by renaming a validation score.

5. Logpool and transport are separate aggregation operators. Current versions normalize over finite, synthetic-only candidate rows. They are explicit approximations with support/diversity limits, not exact continuous logarithmic pools or unregularized Wasserstein barycenters. A best-found weight search is not a global optimality certificate.

6. The exact mixture LP optimizes a fixed empirical pilot query matrix against real validation summaries. Its certificate is conditional on those empirical inputs. Final generated datasets add sampling variability. Own-family nonlinear validation comparators use the same candidate support and fitted operator state.

7. Final test data must not choose model settings, weights, workload, winner, target proportions, or claimed hypotheses. The present per-cell validation selection is a development convenience. A campaign-wide final method must be frozen separately from development validation before confirmation. Official test reuse across partition seeds does not create independent cohorts.

8. The private histogram guarantee is conditional on public fixed record membership, public sizes and a fixed public schema/clipping map. It does not cover data-dependent partition creation unless that mechanism is also protected. Full research bundles include raw public Adult and nonprivate releases; they are not claimed DP. Composing releases across epsilon settings/seeds is mandatory if those releases on overlapping individuals are all published.

9. A separately trained conditional generator is an additional estimator, even when fed samples from the learned joint. Filtering violations is postprocessing, not evidence that the within-condition law is correct. The kernel-smoothed posterior of a finite pool is a separate predictive approximation, not the exact conditional of that finite-support joint on unseen feature rows.
